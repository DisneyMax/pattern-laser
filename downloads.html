Try the sample

  1. Download the reviewed project above, then open the utility.
  2. Click Open project and choose the downloaded JSON.
  3. Inspect Overlay, verify scale, then export the included geometry.

The reviewed project contains five outer contours, 438 stitch-hole contours and one internal cutout. The calibration rectangle geometry is 100 × 20 mm; its canvas includes margins. xTool Studio import and machine processing remain unverified. PDFs opened in the utility are processed in your browser.
